# M6 — Hardening and launch (planned)

Billing with a free tier that is honest (plan, log, comment) and a paid tier that is not a hostage
(reviews, autoregulation, integrations); quota and cost controls; export/import round-trip with a real
30-day delete; observability that answers "why did this change" in one query; a load test at 1,000
concurrent nightly regenerations; subprocessor list, privacy copy, guardian acknowledgement; and a
restore rehearsal.

The restore rehearsal is the criterion this phase exists for: everything before it can be rebuilt from
the repository, and a user's history cannot.

Spine: `spine/m6-launch`.
