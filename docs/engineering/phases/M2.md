# M2 — The daily loop, manual-first (planned)

The loop is where the product either earns the tap or loses it: calendar, five-tap entry with offline
queue and idempotency keys, rollups and signals, the policy evaluator, and the template narrator in both
languages with the LLM off by default.

The gates here are measurements with real runners, not checks. That is why every criterion is
`manual:` — a phase that claims a 30-second median from a unit test is claiming the wrong thing.

Spine: `spine/m2-daily-loop`.
