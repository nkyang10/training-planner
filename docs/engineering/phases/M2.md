# M2 — The daily loop, manual-first (active, 0 of 5)

The loop is where the product either earns the tap or loses it: calendar, five-tap entry with offline
queue and idempotency keys, rollups and signals, the policy evaluator, and the template narrator in both
languages with the LLM off by default.

Opened 2026-11-04, after M1 closed with the planner signed off structurally.

The gates here are measurements with real runners, not checks. That is why every criterion is
`manual:` — a phase that claims a 30-second median from a unit test is claiming the wrong thing. What can
be built and tested before the measurements exist is the loop's pure core: the policy evaluator that turns
a day entry into a structured decision, and the template narrator that turns that decision into a comment
in both languages with the LLM off. Those land first; the measurements then decide whether the loop earns
the tap.
