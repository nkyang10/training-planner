# M3 — Reviews that change the future (planned)

Triggers with dwell, cooldown and hysteresis; the review screen with a keep-as-is option first, a stated
cost, a visible diff and a decline-with-reason; the new immutable version and its stored diff; safety
reviews that cannot be dismissed by a tap; and the weekly summary as the light-touch path.

Every criterion is a human measurement, because "the review was clear" is not a property a schema can
hold. The two-change cap and the freeze window are enforced earlier, in the contract and the DDL, so
they are not repeated here.

Spine: `spine/m3-reviews`.
