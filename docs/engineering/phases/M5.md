# M5 — Autoregulation and calibration (planned)

Wellness baselines with the three-reading rule and sufficiency gating, `rpe_gap`-driven pace-band
recalibration at phase boundaries only, individual ramp-tolerance calibration, comment-flavour
experiments, and the LLM narrative path behind consent with the template floor still tested in CI.

Two criteria are absolute rather than statistical on purpose: the sufficiency gate and the adversarial
suite are the two places where a plausible-looking system quietly does harm.
